About R1 Laura

25+ YEARS OF EXPERIENCE.
ONE STANDARD OF EXCELLENCE.

R1 Laura has been creating and transforming spaces for over 25 years, delivering interior solutions for apartments, individual homes, offices and exterior elevations.

With experience across more than 2500 projects, the company has built its reputation through craftsmanship, execution, attention to detail and long-term customer relationships.

Today, R1 Laura is expanding its capabilities into construction through its dedicated construction division: R1 Laura Constructions — built upon decades of practical experience in design, execution and project coordination.
