Who We Are

BUILDING SPACES,
BUILDING TRUST.

R1 Laura has been creating and transforming spaces for over 25 years, delivering interior solutions for apartments, individual homes, offices and exterior elevations across Visakhapatnam and beyond.

With experience across more than 2500 projects, the company has built its reputation through craftsmanship, execution quality, attention to detail and long-term customer relationships.

What began as a specialized interiors business has grown into a comprehensive design and execution firm with two dedicated divisions:
